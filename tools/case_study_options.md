# Case Study Options for Capstone Project

## Option 1: Bike Share Analysis (Cyclistic)
**Business Question**: How do annual members and casual riders use Cyclistic bikes differently?

**Dataset**: Divvy bike share data (publicly available)
**Focus Areas**: 
- Customer behavior analysis
- Revenue optimization
- Marketing strategy insights

**Skills Demonstrated**:
- Time series analysis
- Customer segmentation
- Geographic analysis
- Business recommendations

---

## Option 2: E-commerce Customer Analysis
**Business Question**: How can we optimize our online store to increase customer lifetime value?

**Dataset**: Customer transaction data, website analytics
**Focus Areas**:
- Customer segmentation
- Purchase behavior patterns
- Churn prediction
- Recommendation systems

**Skills Demonstrated**:
- Customer analytics
- Predictive modeling
- A/B testing analysis
- Business intelligence

---

## Option 3: Healthcare Data Analysis
**Business Question**: What factors contribute to patient readmission rates and how can we improve care?

**Dataset**: Hospital patient data (anonymized)
**Focus Areas**:
- Risk factor identification
- Resource optimization
- Quality improvement
- Outcome prediction

**Skills Demonstrated**:
- Healthcare analytics
- Risk assessment
- Statistical modeling
- Policy recommendations

## Recommendation Process
1. **Assess Interest**: Which domain excites you most?
2. **Data Availability**: Can you access quality data?
3. **Skill Alignment**: Does it showcase your strengths?
4. **Business Value**: Will it demonstrate business impact?
5. **Portfolio Appeal**: Will it impress potential employers?

---

## Next Steps
1. Choose your preferred case study
2. Research data sources and availability
3. Refine the business question
4. Begin data collection and exploration
5. Document your choice and rationale
