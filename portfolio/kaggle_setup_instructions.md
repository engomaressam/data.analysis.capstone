## 📊 Step-by-Step Kaggle Setup

### Step 1: Create Kaggle Account and Notebook

1. **Go to Kaggle**: Visit [kaggle.com/code](https://www.kaggle.com/code)
2. **Create Account**: Sign up or log in to your Kaggle account
3. **New Notebook**: Click **"+ Create"** → **"New Notebook"**
4. **Settings**:
   - **Name**: "Cyclistic Bike Share Analysis - Data Analytics Capstone"
   - **Language**: Python 3
   - **Type**: Notebook (not Script)

### Step 2: Upload Your Data

1. **Create Dataset**:
   - Go to [kaggle.com/datasets](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets)
   - Click **"+ New Dataset"**
   - Upload your cleaned CSV files
   - Name: "Cyclistic Bike Share Data - 2024"
   - Make it **Public**

2. **Add to Notebook**:
   - In your notebook, click **"+ Add Data"**
   - Search for your dataset
   - Add it to your notebook

### Step 3: Copy Notebook Content

1. **Download the Jupyter Notebook**: 
   - File: `portfolio/kaggle_portfolio_notebook.ipynb`
   - This contains the complete Python analysis

2. **Copy Content to Kaggle**:
   - Copy each cell from the notebook
   - Paste into your Kaggle notebook
   - Run cells to verify they work

### Step 4: Customize for Your Data

Replace the sample data loading with your actual data:

```python
# Replace this sample data loading:
sample_data = pd.DataFrame({...})

# With your actual data loading:
cyclistic_data = pd.read_csv('/kaggle/input/your-dataset-name/your-file.csv')
```
