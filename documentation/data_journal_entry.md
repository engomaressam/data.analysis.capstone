## Project Context: Cyclistic Bike Share Analysis

**Business Question**: How do annual members and casual riders use Cyclistic bikes differently?

**Background**: 
Cyclistic is a bike-share program that features more than 5,800 bicycles and 600 docking stations. Cyclistic sets itself apart by also offering reclining bikes, hand tricycles, and cargo bikes, making bike-share more inclusive to people with disabilities and riders who can't use a standard two-wheeled bike. The majority of riders opt for traditional bikes; about 8% of riders use the assistive options. Cyclistic users are more likely to ride for leisure, but about 30% use them to commute to work each day.

**Key Stakeholders**:
- **Lily Moreno**: Director of Marketing
- **Cyclistic Executive Team**: Decision makers
- **Cyclistic Marketing Analytics Team**: Data analysts

**Business Goal**: Design marketing strategies aimed at converting casual riders into annual members.

**Key Questions**:
1. How do annual members and casual riders use Cyclistic bikes differently?
2. Why would casual riders buy Cyclistic annual memberships?
3. How can Cyclistic use digital media to influence casual riders to become members?

## Data Sources

**Primary Dataset**: Divvy's historical trip data (publicly available)
- **Location**: [Chicago Data Portal](https://www.divvybikes.com/system-data)
- **Format**: CSV files by month
- **Time Period**: Last 12 months of data
- **License**: [Divvy Data License Agreement](https://www.divvybikes.com/data-license-agreement)

**Data Description**:
The data has been pruned and anonymized by Cyclistic for privacy protection. The data includes:
- Ride ID
- Rideable type (bike type)
- Started at (start time and date)
- Ended at (end time and date)
- Start station name
- Start station ID
- End station name
- End station ID
- Start latitude/longitude
- End latitude/longitude
- Member casual (rider type)
